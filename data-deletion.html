User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Pupper's automated posting application does not collect or store personal data from any users, followers, or third parties. The application only accesses Pupper's own Facebook and Instagram business account credentials to publish content.

What Data Exists

The only data stored by this application is:

  • Facebook Page access tokens for Pupper's own business accounts
  • Instagram Business Account identifiers for Pupper's own accounts

No user data, follower data, or personal information from any third party is ever collected or stored.

To Request Data Deletion

If you are a Facebook or Instagram user who believes this application has accessed your data, please contact us and we will investigate and remove any associated data within 30 days.

Email: aj@portraitpal.ai

Subject line: Data Deletion Request

You may also revoke this application's access at any time through your Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.
