Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pupper ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our automated social media posting application.

1. What This App Does

This application automates the posting of content to Pupper's own Instagram and TikTok business accounts. It does not collect, store, or process data from any users other than the business owner.

2. Data We Access

To post content on behalf of Pupper's social accounts, the application accesses:

  • Facebook Page access tokens (stored securely as encrypted environment variables)
  • Instagram Business Account identifiers (used only to publish content)

We do not collect personal data from any third parties, followers, or visitors.

3. How We Store Data

All API credentials and tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in GitHub Actions and are never logged, shared, or transmitted to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

This app integrates with Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok APIs solely to publish brand content. Their respective privacy policies govern how those platforms handle data.
